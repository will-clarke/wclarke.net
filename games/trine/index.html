How to play

  • drag anywhere to draw a mirror line - the shard flips across it, and the ghost preview shows the landing before you let go.
  • Stroke near a placed mirror to redraw just that one - the whole chain of flips recomputes live as you drag. Nothing is ever locked in.
  • Land the shard exactly on the faint target. Lines snap to neat angles and quarter-steps; when the result fits, it locks gold.
  • Every flip swaps the shard's handedness - some targets need an even count of flips, some an odd one.
  • Faint outlines show each stop along the chain. A stop that touches a void or leaves the board glows red - the level won't close until every flip is clean.
  • Sweep over ✦ stars along the way. par is the fewest mirrors - gold dot at par. Any pose is at most three flips away; errands cost extra.

Trine

Drag a mirror. The shard flips. Any pose is three flips away - at most.
