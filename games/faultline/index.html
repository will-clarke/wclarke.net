Faultline

FAULTLINE

HOW TO PLAY

  • Drag a row or column. It slides one tile and wraps around the edge. 3 shifts a turn; UNDO is free until you end the turn.
  • Round units ride the ground. Square units are anchored - the ground slides beneath them. Drag lava under a brute.
  • A rider shoved into an anchored unit stops. Both take 1 crush.
  • The rogue jabs adjacent enemies for 1 whenever her line shifts.
  • END TURN: you strike, the ground burns (lava 2, spikes 1), they bite, they advance. Red haze marks their reach.
  • 🛡️ Knight - 6 HP, 2 dmg to all adjacent
  • 🏹 Archer - 3 HP, 1 dmg down each clear lane
  • 🗡️ Rogue - 4 HP, 1 dmg adjacent + jab on shift
  • 🗿 Golem - 8 HP, 2 dmg adjacent, anchored
  • 👹 Grunt - 3 HP, bites for 1
  • 👿 Brute - 6 HP, bites for 2, anchored, slow
