How to play

  • drag inside the veil - the dark wedge - and your seed of light blooms into every copy the kaleidoscope makes. Let go to plant it.
  • The faint rings are the target. Under the veil it is hidden - infer it from the copies you can see. Match the whole pattern exactly to win.
  • tap a planted seed to unplant it; drag one to move it and its whole bloom follows. Copies landing where the target isn't turn pink.
  • C5 means five turns; D5 adds mirrors. A seed placed exactly on a fold makes half as many copies - and the still centre makes just one.
  • par is the number of families in the target - one seed each. In late levels the scope is unlabelled: pick it, then paint. Solve at par for gold.
